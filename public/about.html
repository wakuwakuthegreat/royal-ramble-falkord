How the whole thing fits together

A court game where nobody follows a script. Every person has a nature, a memory and a grudge; the rest is what they do about it.

One season the only clock there is

You get one move at a time, but the world gets four a year. When a season turns, everything below happens in order, to you and to everyone else.

People who they are decides what they do

🎭

Traits

Two or three each: ambitious, kind, wrathful, pious, deceitful… They shift every choice below, and colour how the character speaks.

💞

Regard

How each person feels about each other person, from sworn enemy to devoted. Family starts warm. Quarrels, friendships and sins move it.

🙂

Opinion of you

What they think of you, built from gifts, promises, insults, your prestige, the company you keep, and what they know about your deeds.

🧠

Memory

What you say and do is written to a FalkorDB memory graph. The next conversation, or the next ruler, remembers it.

Nobody is "the villain". An ambitious second son with a cruel streak and a king past sixty is a story waiting to be told.

Schemes drives become plans

Each season, every adult weighs what they want against what they are. Grudge, jealousy, an heir's patience, greed, desire. Sometimes the answer is a plan. You, your courtiers and foreign rulers all use the same rules.

You run these from the 🗝 Intrigue window, or from any character's page. You can do the deed yourself or send an agent: a spymaster takes the fall instead of you, if they keep quiet.

Secrets everything leaves a trace

Plots, crimes, affairs, forged papers and bastards all leave a secret: a fact, true or false, and a list of who knows it. Your dossier holds the ones you know.

How secrets move

  1. Whispers. People tell their friends. Gossips tell more.
  2. Conscience. The pious and honest struggle to keep a grave secret.
  3. Spite. An enemy of the guilty may simply tell everyone.
  4. Crowds. Five people know? It is out by Tuesday.

What you can do with one

  1. Expose it. It becomes public; the guilty carry the mark.
  2. Blackmail. Gold, once or twice. The proud hit back.
  3. Verify it. Rumours may be lies. Spreading a false one backfires.
  4. Keep it. A secret in hand is leverage later.

Getting information

🧙

Your spymaster

Hears things by himself. A sharper one hears more. Ask for a sweep when you are worried.

🕵

Spies

Place them in other courts. They report secrets and news. They can be caught, and foreign spies sit in yours.

🗣

Gossip

Ask someone what they know. Friends talk. The deceitful may invent. Friends also warn you unprompted.

⛓

Prisoners

An arrested plotter can be questioned: secrets, and the names of the helpers.

Sins & a good name public deeds stick

While a deed is secret nothing happens. When it comes out, the doer gets a mark. Everyone who knew them thinks less of them, the victim's kin never forgive, and a ruler's throne gets shakier. If the doer is you, every ruler's opinion of you drops, for as long as you carry it. The pious mind most. The cynical and ambitious barely blink.

🗡 Murderer🩸 Kinslayer🩸 Regicide🕯 Incestuous💔 Adulterer📜 Forger🐍 Slanderer💰 Thief⛓ Traitor

A mark can be paid off with penance: gold, a priest, a barefoot walk. It fades the stain, not the memory.

War every war has a reason

⚔

Conquest

Land, plain and simple.

☩

Holy war

Against a neighbour of another faith. The pious cheer and co-believers may march with you.

👑

A claim

Real or forged. A won claim carries a second province. A forged one can be exposed.

🚪

Restoration

Shelter a deposed ruler, then put them back on their throne. They will owe you everything.

Grand wars. When a war starts, each side's allies decide whether to join. Their armies add to the battle and bleed with it. Hold an alliance with someone on the far side and you will be asked to pick.

You fight one battle a season and choose the tactic. Take the score to ±100 to win or lose the province. Peace is possible any time, on terms the score allows.

Rise & fall nothing stays the same size

Conquered realms fall off the map. Rebel realms are born onto it. Your own people can do it to you: starve them, tax them to the bone, execute too freely, and a revolt arrives. You can listen, buy them off, or send the army. If you lose, a piece of your kingdom leaves.

Talking words, or buttons. Your choice

Every action in the game is a button. Talking to someone is an additional way to do the same things, with a living character in the room.

In conversation you can: ask for gold, make a promise, pay in person, ask for a secret, ask someone to join your council or your plot, confront a suspect, or quietly ask your spymaster to arrange a death. Characters keep their own secrets. A plotter will deny it to your face unless you hold proof, or they are the sort to crack.

Shared worlds one world, many rulers

⏳

A real-time clock

Seasons turn by themselves. Alone and reading a popup, the sand runs three times slower.

🗣

Stand-ins

Step away and a steward speaks for you, guided by what you told it.

🔒

Private knowledge

You are only ever told the plots and secrets you have found. Other players cannot be targets of your schemes: write them a letter.

📜

Tales from afar

Big deeds are written to shared memory. Other players hear of them as gossip.

A few tips for a first reign

  • Appoint a spymaster who is good at it. It is the cheapest defence there is.
  • Be kind to people who might warn you. They do.
  • A younger child named as heir makes a loyal friend and a dangerous brother.
  • If you must have someone killed, do it through an agent, and hope they are loyal.
  • Check a rumour before you spread it.
